## Why this shape (decisions to keep)

1. **Decompose, don't reinterpret.** The typeable path records what the
   user physically did: keystrokes → TextEntry card with `typedValue`,
   option click → Click card. Flow B (type + Enter commit) yields ONE
   TextEntry; Flow C (type + click-away) yields TextEntry + Click.
2. **Readonly stays Dropdown.** OXD-style display inputs (readOnly=true)
   are still claimable by Dropdown — E2E Flow D pinned (`status-d` →
   Dropdown, zero TextEntry). This is the R1 contract from B1/6D.1.
3. **DatePicker guard is deliberately narrow.** `listbox` only. A broad
   role=combobox disclaim would have broken MUI DatePicker (dialog popup).
4. **IR honesty over replay coverage.** Two CLICK steps are replayable
   everywhere; a SELECT against a div-option combobox is replayable
   nowhere. Honesty > coverage (doctrine).
5. **Accept costs (pinned in spec §6)**: DIV combobox typing not yet
   decomposed (Flow-A on div triggers still Dropdown — R2 unchanged);
   `aria-activedescendant` keyboard-only selection has no click event →
   no IR step for the commit (input/change not discrete); datalist native
   option selection produces no option click; S1 fields are advisory
   signals, not claim gates.

## The capture-model audit (owner-directed, read-only, same day)

Question: should Click classification become
"capture every genuine click → exclude only demonstrable non-actionable
clicks → understand later"? Conclusion: **the architecture is already
capture-first** at the preservation layers (tap → ledger → projection
preserves every discrete event as Click OR Unclassified); qualification
gates only the label and the IR step boundary, which is the honesty
invariant itself. Reversal would fabricate actionhood (label without
evidence), degrade replay (nameless-div locators), and destroy the KR
cross-session learning signal. Full reasoning in the roadmap B2 entry and
the session log; recommendation adopted: keep the model, fix the two REAL
silent-loss channels it surfaced —
  (1) accumulating `input`/`change` events never enter the ledger → typed
      text with no completing lifecycle is silently lost (same class B2
      fixed for comboboxes, still open generically);
  (2) structural targets (`BODY`/`HTML`/`SVG` internals) return null from
      `resolveTarget` → click-away dismissals never enter the ledger.
Plus a policy inconsistency worth resolving: `output-adapter.toIRAction`
maps Unclassified click → CLICK (`unclassified:true`) while `ir-bridge`
drops all Unclassified (NOISE_TYPES).
